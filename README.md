# IDKWTP — I Don't Know What To Play
A World of Warcraft class/spec/race randomizer with filters, a slot machine, personal & group modes, and best-of-3/5.

Live: https://grokbot-daniel.github.io/idkwtp/

Supports two game versions (toggle): **WoW: Forever** (Classic+, launches Nov 4 2026; 9 classes, 10 race options, 56 combos) and **Retail (Midnight)** (13 classes, 40 specs incl. Devourer DH, 26 races incl. Haranir, 243 combos).
